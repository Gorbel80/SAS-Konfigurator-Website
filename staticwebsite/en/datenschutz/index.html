Privacy policy

We inform you about the processing of personal data when using this website (GDPR). The information matches the current functionality of the site.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Controller

The controller for data processing related to this website is Wima Industrie Automation GmbH (see legal notice). Contact details are given in the legal notice.

2. Hosting and server logs

When you visit the site, the hosting provider (ALL-INKL.COM – Neue Medien Münnich) processes technically necessary data in server logs (including IP address, date/time, requested file, user agent, referrer). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and stable operation). Data is typically stored only briefly and is not merged with other data sources.

3. Cookies and consent

We use essential cookies/storage (e.g. your cookie choice in local storage) to provide the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, Section 25(2) TDDDG). Non-essential cookies (e.g. analytics) are only set if you choose “Accept all” in the cookie banner (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). You can change your decision at any time by deleting the local storage key “sas_cookie_consent” and reloading the page.

4. Contact form and email

If you contact us via the form or by email, we process the data you provide (e.g. name, company, email, phone, message) to handle your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual measures, or (f) for general interest). The form opens your local email application; transmission is handled by your email provider. Retention: as long as processing requires, then deletion unless statutory retention obligations apply.

5. Recipients and processors

Technical service providers (hosting, possibly email transport) may act as processors under Art. 28 GDPR. Transfers to third countries may occur when using international cloud providers; in that case appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses) are sought. Details depend on the hosting actually used.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Consents may be withdrawn at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

7. Obligation to provide data

Providing personal data is partly technically necessary to visit the website (log files). For contact requests, the data needed to respond is required; without it we may not be able to process your enquiry.

8. Updates

We reserve the right to adjust this privacy policy if the website, the legal situation or our processes change.
