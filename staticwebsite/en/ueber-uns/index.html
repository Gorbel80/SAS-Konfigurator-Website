Operating data acquisition

Digital service – directly in the hoist

Operating data acquisition, maintenance documentation and manufacturer proof – without APP or cloud lock-in – directly in the device.

LIFT-LINK brings order into the device and simplifies documentation.

On contactor-controlled units the module is installed directly in the device. Because control voltage is not uniform, LIFT-LINK detects the control voltage. Whether 15 or 60 V DC or 12–42 V AC – these voltages are processed automatically. Once installed, LIFT-LINK records all relevant parameters.
